================================================================================
PLETHORA ERP — WHATSAPP USER GUIDE FOR OFFICE STAFF
Quick, Easy & Mobile Daily Attendance, Leave, Payslips & Office Services
================================================================================

1. INTRODUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Welcome to Plethora ERP on WhatsApp. 

As an office staff member (Administration, HR, Finance, Operations, Logistics,
Management, or Support), you can manage your daily workday attendance, check
leave balances, apply for leave, download payslips, and request assistance directly
from your smartphone through WhatsApp.

There is no need to install new apps or log into complex web portals for routine
daily tasks.


2. GETTING STARTED & PREREQUISITES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before you can interact with Plethora on WhatsApp:

1. Registered Phone Number:
   Ensure your mobile phone number is registered with your HR / Payroll
   department in your Plethora employee profile. Plethora automatically links
   your WhatsApp number to your employee profile.

2. Company WhatsApp Contact:
   Save your company's official Plethora WhatsApp number in your phone contacts
   (e.g., "Company Office Assistant" or "Plethora").

3. Starting the Chat:
   Open WhatsApp, find the contact, and send any greeting:
   • Type: hi, hello, menu, or help

   Plethora will immediately reply with your interactive main menu.


3. THE MAIN MENU & QUICK SHORTCUTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When you send "menu" or "help", Plethora displays the main command list. You can
tap an interactive button, or simply reply with any number from 1 to 8:

  [1] Clock In          — Start your office workday
  [2] Clock Out         — Finish your office workday and record hours
  [3] Report Incident   — Log office maintenance, hazards, or security issues
  [4] Work Schedule     — View your contracted working days and hours
  [5] Latest Payslip    — Receive your latest monthly payslip as a PDF
  [6] Leave Services    — Apply for leave, check balances, or track requests
  [7] Office Status     — View today's attendance time, schedule, and pending items
  [8] Contact HR/Support— Request assistance from HR or management

* Pro-Tip: You don't need to navigate through menus. You can simply send "1" to 
  clock in in the morning and "2" to clock out when leaving!


4. DAILY ATTENDANCE (CLOCK IN & CLOCK OUT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. CLOCKING IN (MORNING)
   When you arrive at work or begin your workday:
   • Send: 1  (or type: clock in, in)

   Plethora will verify your status and confirm:
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Good day, Nomsa! 🏢

   You are clocked in for today at 08:02 (HR Officer).

   Have a productive day! Reply *2* or *clock out* when you finish work.
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

   Important Notes on Clocking In:
   • Leave Protection: If you have approved annual or sick leave scheduled for
     today, Plethora will block accidental clock-ins and notify you:
     "❌ You are on approved annual leave today. Contact HR if your dates changed."
   • Already Clocked In: If you already clocked in earlier, Plethora will remind
     you of your clock-in time rather than creating duplicates.


B. CLOCKING OUT (AFTERNOON / END OF DAY)
   When your workday is complete:
   • Send: 2  (or type: clock out, out)

   Plethora will calculate your total hours worked for the day and confirm:
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
   🏢 *Clock Out Confirmed*

   • Clock In: 08:02
   • Clock Out: 17:05
   • Hours Worked: 9.05 hrs

   Have a great evening, Nomsa!
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

   Important Notes on Clocking Out:
   • If you haven't clocked in today, Plethora will prompt you to clock in first.
   • If you already clocked out earlier, Plethora will show your completed summary.


5. CHECKING YOUR STATUS & WORKING SCHEDULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. CHECKING YOUR CURRENT STATUS
   To see whether you are currently clocked in, how long you've been working today,
   and if you have any pending leave requests:
   • Send: 7  (or type: status)

   Example Response:
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
   📊 *Your Office Status*

   👤 *Today's Attendance*:
   🟢 *Active at Office*
     Clocked in: 08:02 (4h 15m elapsed)

   🕒 *Working Schedule*:
   📅 *Office Schedule*:
     Monday – Friday (40 hours/week, 08:00 - 17:00)

   📋 *Leave Requests*:
   • No pending leave applications

   Reply *1* to Clock In, *2* to Clock Out, *6* for Leave, or *menu* for all options.
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------------


B. VIEWING YOUR WORK SCHEDULE
   • Send: 4  (or type: roster, schedule)

   Because office personnel work standard business schedules rather than rotating
   security guard shifts, Plethora provides your contracted work details:
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
   📅 *Office Staff Work Schedule*

   You are registered as office staff (HR Officer).
   Office staff follow standard working schedules rather than site-based shifts.

   • *Working Days*: Monday – Friday
   • *Working Hours*: 08:00 – 17:00 (40 hours/week)

   Reply *1* to Clock In or *2* to Clock Out for your workday.
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------------


6. LEAVE SERVICES (BALANCES, APPLICATIONS & SICK NOTES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. CHECKING LEAVE BALANCES
   Want to know how many days of Annual, Sick, or Family leave you have available?
   • Send: leave balance  (or send: balance)

   Plethora replies with your up-to-date statutory balances in days.


B. CHECKING RECENT LEAVE APPLICATIONS
   To check the status of recent leave requests (Approved, Pending, or Rejected):
   • Send: leave status  (or send: leave history)


C. APPLYING FOR LEAVE (GUIDED MICRO-PROMPT)
   Applying for leave is a simple step-by-step guided conversation:
   1. Send: 6  (or send: leave, apply leave)
   2. Choose leave type:
      Reply 1 for Annual Leave
      Reply 2 for Sick Leave
      Reply 3 for Family Responsibility Leave
      Reply 4 for Study Leave
   3. Enter start date:
      Type the start date (e.g. 2026-10-05, or "today", or "tomorrow")
   4. Enter end date:
      Type the end date (e.g. 2026-10-09, or press 1 if single day)
   5. Enter reason (optional):
      Type a brief note (e.g. "Family wedding") or reply "skip"
   6. Confirm:
      Plethora summarizes the request and working days. Reply "1" to confirm.
      Your request is immediately submitted to management for review!

   * Quick One-Liner Shortcut:
     You can also apply in a single line if you prefer:
     • leave 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-09 annual Family holiday


D. SUBMITTING MEDICAL CERTIFICATES / SICK NOTES
   If you applied for sick leave and need to provide a doctor's note:
   1. Take a clear photo of your medical certificate or locate the PDF document.
   2. Send the image/PDF to the WhatsApp chat with the caption:
      leave <REQUEST_NUMBER>
      Example caption: leave REQ-1042
   3. Plethora will automatically attach the document to your leave record and
      notify HR for verification.


E. WITHDRAWING A LEAVE APPLICATION
   If your plans change and your leave request has not yet been taken:
   • Send: leave withdraw <REQUEST_NUMBER>
     Example: leave withdraw REQ-1042


7. DOWNLOADING MONTHLY PAYSLIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can download your latest official payslip directly to WhatsApp:
• Send: 5  (or type: payslip, pay slip)

Plethora will locate your latest confirmed payroll run and send your payslip
directly as a PDF document. You can open, save, or print it right from your phone.


8. REPORTING OFFICE INCIDENTS OR MAINTENANCE ISSUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you notice an office safety hazard, facility defect, broken air conditioning,
water leak, electrical fault, or security concern:
1. Send: 3  (or type: incident, report)
2. Select Category:
   [1] Emergency / Safety Hazard
   [2] Break-in / Theft / Loss
   [3] Staff Misconduct
   [4] Office Damage / Facility Maintenance
   [5] Other / General Complaint
3. Provide Details:
   Type a clear description (e.g. "Main board room aircon leaking water onto carpet")
4. Attach Photos:
   Plethora will prompt you to send photos. Simply take a photo of the issue and
   send it. It will be attached as evidence and dispatched to facility management.


9. CONTACTING HR / SUPERVISOR ESCALATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you need direct assistance from HR, payroll, or an office administrator:
• Send: 8  (or type: supervisor, support, help me)

Plethora will:
1. Generate an official assistance reference code: e.g. SUP-20260924-1234
2. Alert the HR/Operations desk immediately.
3. Reply confirming that an administrator has been notified to follow up with you.


10. UNIVERSAL COMMANDS & HELPFUL TIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• CANCEL ANY TIME:
  If you are in the middle of applying for leave or reporting an incident and
  want to stop, simply type:
  cancel, exit, stop, or 0.
  Plethora will reset back to the main menu.

• AUTOMATIC SESSION TIMEOUT:
  If you leave a conversation unfinished for more than 15 minutes, Plethora
  automatically clears the pending step so your next message starts fresh.

• PAYROLL PROTECTION GUARANTEE:
  Office staff are compensated on a fixed monthly salary. WhatsApp attendance
  tracking exists for HR daily roll calls, leave reconciliation, and safety
  auditing. Daily clock-in/out timestamps will NEVER alter or deduct from your
  fixed basic monthly salary.

• NEED TO UPDATE YOUR PHONE NUMBER?
  If you change your cell number, contact your HR department to update your
  Plethora profile so your WhatsApp access remains active.
